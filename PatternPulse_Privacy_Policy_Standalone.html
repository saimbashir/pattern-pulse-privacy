At a glance: The main game works with a locally stored player profile. The Android app also integrates Google AdMob, so the advertising service can process certain information even though the developer does not run an online player-account system. This policy explains both.
SECTION 01

Overview

Pattern Pulse (the “App”) is a memory and pattern game developed by Saim Bashir (“we,” “our,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App and how third-party advertising may work.

The App does not require you to register an online account. Game progress is designed to be stored on your device. The App includes a third-party advertising SDK that may collect and share information separately for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.

SECTION 02

Information and storage

Information you provide and information stored in the App: When you start playing, you enter a player name. Pattern Pulse stores that name together with your level progress, completed levels, attempts, in-game coin balance, power-up inventory, and reward eligibility in a local SQLite database on your device. Your player name is not required to be your real name.

We have not implemented a developer-operated player account, cloud synchronization service, or a feature that uploads this local player profile to our own server in the reviewed version of the App.

If you email us: We receive the email address and any information you choose to include in your message so that we can respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information unnecessarily.

Important distinction

Locally saved game information and information processed by Google AdMob are different. Even if you do not enter a real name or create an account, the advertising SDK may handle device information described in the next section.

SECTION 03

Advertising and Google AdMob

The Android App includes Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner advertisements and optional rewarded video advertisements. You can choose not to watch a rewarded ad and continue playing without that optional reward. Ad availability depends on the device, network, ad inventory, and app configuration.

When the advertising SDK is active, Google may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location (not precise GPS location).
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostics and performance information, such as launch time, hang rate, and energy use.
  • Device and account identifiers, potentially including the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable identifiers, depending on the device, permissions, SDK settings, and available controls.

Google may use this information to deliver and measure ads, analyze performance, and detect fraud or abuse. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on the configuration, applicable requirements, your settings, and any required consent. The App's current version does not include an in-app advertising-consent or preference screen; this policy is not a substitute for a consent mechanism where one is required.

For Google's practices, see Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure documentation.

SECTION 04

How information is used and shared

Local game data is used to remember your player profile, maintain your progress, award earned coins, and manage power-ups. The reviewed App does not contain a feature that sends your local game profile to our own server or sells that profile.

Advertising-related information may be transmitted to Google and its advertising partners through the AdMob SDK for ad delivery, measurement, service improvement, and fraud prevention in accordance with their terms and applicable settings. We do not control Google's independent processing or retention of advertising-related information.

We may also process information you voluntarily send in an email to respond to you.

SECTION 05

Your choices and controls

  • Rewarded ads: You can select “No thanks” or otherwise skip an optional rewarded ad. You will not receive the ad-based bonus if you skip it.
  • Advertising settings: You can review the advertising and privacy controls available in your Android device settings and in Google My Ad Center. Available controls depend on your account, device, and region.
  • Local data: You can clear the App's storage through Android settings or uninstall the App to remove locally saved progress from the device. Clearing storage or uninstalling can permanently remove your progress and coins.
  • Questions or requests: Contact us using the email address below. Requests concerning data controlled by Google may also need to be directed to Google through its own privacy controls.

Not every ad-related collection or processing activity can be disabled simply by skipping rewarded videos. Any required advertising consent and privacy controls must also be handled within the App and its SDK configuration.

SECTION 06

Retention and deletion

Game profile, progression, coins, and inventory remain in the App's local database while the App's stored data remains on your device. The current “Reset levels” action resets level progress but does not delete your player name, coin balance, or inventory. To delete all locally stored App data, clear the App's storage in Android settings or uninstall the App. The Android build is configured not to allow Android app-data backup.

If you contact us by email, we retain the correspondence as needed to respond to the inquiry and handle related follow-up, subject to applicable requirements. You may email us to request deletion of correspondence within our control.

Google manages retention and deletion of data it processes through AdMob under its own policies and controls. Deleting the App does not necessarily delete information already held by third parties.

SECTION 07

Security

Your game profile is stored in app-private device storage, and Android app-data backup is disabled in the reviewed configuration. The App does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone as part of its intended functionality.

Google states that information transmitted by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. We aim to use reasonable safeguards, but no mobile device, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

SECTION 08

Children's privacy

Pattern Pulse does not ask players to provide an email address, date of birth, or online account to play. It does ask for a local player name and integrates third-party advertising as described above. We do not knowingly solicit additional personal information from children through the game's own features.

Where the App is offered to children, additional age-appropriate ad serving, SDK configuration, data restrictions, and parental-consent requirements may apply. Parents or guardians with privacy questions can contact us using the email below.

SECTION 09

Third-party services

Google AdMob is the advertising service integrated into this version of Pattern Pulse. Advertisements may lead to third-party websites or applications that operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies before sharing information with those services.
